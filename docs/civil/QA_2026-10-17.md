## California staff workspace batch (not yet deployed)

Added a separate California staff workspace on the existing Civil board, assignments,
calendar, party search and reports routes. It uses standard agency roles and shared
`civil.papers` records, rather than the Platform Admin-only summons demonstration table.
Dashboard intake and My Work case links route to the California workspace.

Supported case-management paths: summons, small claims, subpoena, restraining order,
earnings withholding, execution, bank levy, possession, personal property levy,
real property levy and sheriff sale. These are workflow scaffolds with manually
reviewed packet requirements. They do not implement every statute-specific notice,
exemption deadline, service method restriction, sale distribution or generated court
form for each case type. Uploaded completed returns require independent review.
No legal deadline or fee is calculated from Arizona defaults.

Server safeguards: tenant/agency membership and licensing; own-assignment visibility;
role abilities; version checks under row lock; audit and case updates in one
transaction; actual uploaded-object ownership verification; independent return and
payment approval; claims/stays preventing enforcement and payouts; integer cents,
insufficient-fund checks, receipt replay protection, final reconciliation; immutable
closed cases. External payments and court delivery are recorded using references;
the application does not send payments or file with the court.

## Release-control batch following PR #53

This batch adds:

- Cursor pagination (200 cases/page) and server-side case/party/address search.
  Own-assignment scoping is applied by SQL before pagination. Accounting totals
  cover every accessible case, rather than the former 1,000-case window.
- Accounting-date range summaries and complete case/ledger CSV exports. Exports
  compare a hash of accessible record keys/versions before and after retrieval;
  a changed dataset blocks download rather than producing inconsistent output.
- Explicit tracked deadlines with authority and completion references, and notice
  delivery records linked to uploaded evidence. These capture reviewed dates;
  they do not automatically resolve every workflow's statutory date calculation.
- Undelivered return correction, preserving the earlier review in history.
- Sonoma levy collection funds held for at least 21 calendar days. Longer active
  claims/stays and unresolved tracked exemption deadlines also block payouts.
  Released funds are shown separately from the total case balance. This is a
  case ledger, not a replacement for agency treasury/bank reconciliation.
- Published Sonoma fee reference amounts shown in Jurisdiction configuration.
  They do not automatically determine exemptions or charge a customer.
- Editable unsigned POS-010 and SC-104 drafts for personal service on the named
  individual. Templates came from the official California Courts download URLs;
  source and normalized template SHA-256 values are recorded in
  `lambdas/api/templates/civil/manifest.json`. XFA is removed to keep a single
  editable AcroForm representation. Certifications, signing dates and signatures
  are left blank. Each generation records document/template hashes in case
  history and participates in the existing case/audit transaction. Users complete
  remaining items, review, sign and upload the final proof before return review.
  Other methods, entities/agents and other workflows still require the
  appropriate completed official form.
- A version/county-bound release evidence checklist in Jurisdiction configuration.
  Platform administrators record references and accepted workflows. Agency
  administrators can read it. Stale revisions are rejected, updates are audited
  atomically, and evidence completion does not enable operational activation.

Local verification includes 1,106 PostgreSQL case records to prove pagination and
report coverage past the old cap; accounting date boundaries; checksum-based
export rejection after concurrent changes; actual report controls and CSV output
under jsdom; readiness authorization/revision/rollback tests; fund maturation,
receipt/payment replay controls, deadline/notice evidence and return correction.
Official-form tests verify editable canonical values, unchecked certifications and
blank signing dates. Both generated forms were rendered and visually inspected,

The source check now runs `tests/civil-california-release-controls.test.mjs`.
