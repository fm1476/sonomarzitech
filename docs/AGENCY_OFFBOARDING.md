# Agency Data Export and Offboarding Runbook

## Purpose

This runbook defines the minimum controlled process for exporting and offboarding an agency from SonoMarzi.

## Principles

- The agency's data must remain scoped to its tenant and agency identifiers.
- Export must not contain another agency's records or attachments.
- Access is disabled only after the export is prepared and validated unless the agency requests immediate suspension.
- Destructive deletion is never performed from an informal request or support message.

## Offboarding workflow

1. Validate the requestor and written authorization.
2. Record the tenant ID, agency ID, agency name, requested export format, requested cutoff date, and requested disposition.
3. Freeze non-essential administrative changes during the export window.
4. Export all RDS records for exactly the tenant/agency pair, including deleted/tombstoned records when required for audit history.
5. Export the agency's attachment objects from the tenant/agency S3 prefix.
6. Export audit history, user/membership information, configuration, roles, Field Training data, workflows, notices, and module records.
7. Generate a manifest with record count, object count, export timestamp, tenant ID, agency ID, and checksums for packaged files.
8. Validate the export against known counts and inspect samples from multiple modules.
9. Deliver using an approved secure transfer mechanism.
10. Obtain receipt/acceptance from the agency.
11. Disable agency user access and integrations.
12. Apply the contracted retention period.
13. When authorized retention expires, perform deletion according to the contract and record evidence of completion.

## Minimum export contents

- Tenant and agency configuration.
- Personnel and membership data.
- Roles and abilities.
- Quartermaster.
- Fleet.
- Personnel Management.
- K9.
- Drone/UAS.
- EOD.
- Subpoenas.
- Grants/forfeiture.
- Civil Process.
- Licensing & Permits.
- Field Training.
- Workflows and approvals.
- Staff Notices where contractually included.
- Audit history.
- Attachment files and attachment metadata.

## Validation rule

An export is not complete until the exported tenant/agency identifiers have been checked and no records from another tenant or agency are present.

## Current implementation status

SonoMarzi has module-level report exports, but a complete automated whole-agency export package is not yet implemented. Until that feature exists, this runbook is the controlled operational process and the export should be generated by an authorized Platform Admin using server-side tenant/agency filters.
